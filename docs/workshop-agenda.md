# Workshop Agenda (Facilitated Day)

This page is the **map for the facilitated workshop day**. The course modules in the sidebar are
numbered **00–13** for self-paced study; on the day we run a **re-sequenced subset**, grouped into
**Missions**.

> 🧭 **Online vs. on the day.** The online course is complete and self-contained — if you're working
> through it at the office, start at [Module 00](modules/00-course-setup/) and go in order. On the
> facilitated day, your environment is **already provisioned**, so we skip setup and start building.

---

## What's pre-provisioned (so we can skip it on the day)

- **Module 00 — Course Setup:** your tenant, the **IT Help Desk** SharePoint site, the **Tickets**
  list, the knowledge documents, and publishing security are **already set up** in your workshop
  environment. (Revisit [Module 00](modules/00-course-setup/) at the office to do this yourself.)
- **Module 01 — Introduction to Agents** and **Module 02 — New Copilot Studio Fundamentals** are
  covered as **presentation**, not hands-on labs. Read them online anytime for the full concepts.

## Day activities that aren't numbered modules

- **Free Play Challenge** (end of Mission #4) — open time to extend Bit: add a skill, a card, or a
  tool of your choosing.
- **Plan Your First Agent** (Mission Complete) — sketch an agent for a real problem on your own team.
- **Keep building** — where to go next, and how to claim your badge.
